How It Works

  • Join solo or in a team of 2: Enter as an individual meme author or dynamic pair.
  • Everyone gets the same theme or situation each round: Synchronized challenge prompt announced on stage.
  • Create an AI-generated meme with a well-crafted prompt: Utilize image and text generation models with smart styling.
  • Finish within the fixed time limit: Sprints last 5 to 10 minutes per round.
  • Memes are displayed to the audience and judges: Real-time reactions, laughs, and live evaluation.
