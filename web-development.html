Official Regulations

Rules

  • Individual or team participation: Compete solo or form a team of up to 4 members.
  • Timing: 2 – 3 Hours: Build and complete your project strictly within the 2 to 3 hour competition window. Late submissions will not be evaluated.
  • Topic is revealed at the start: Coordinator announces the surprise domain at the opening whistle.
  • AI tools and online resources are allowed: Use LLMs, code assistants, UI libraries, or CSS frameworks.
  • Coordinator's and judges' decision is final: Standard collegiate fair play is enforced.
Evaluation Metrics

You Are Judged On

  • 1. UI / UX Visual Hierarchy
  • 2. Creativity Original Thinking
  • 3. Functionality Working Interactivity
  • 4. Overall presentation Demo & Pitch
