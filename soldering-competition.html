Round Breakdown

Three Progressive Rounds (45 Min)

The competition tests your practical soldering skills, accuracy, component handling, and ability to assemble working electronic circuits with rising difficulty.

Round 1 10 Minutes

Basic Soldering Challenge

Participants solder 1–2 electronic components to create a simple circuit. Evaluation focuses strictly on basic soldering technique, joint cleanliness, and neat connections.

Focus: Clean solder joints & heat control
Round 2 15 Minutes

Perfboard Challenge

Participants solder multiple electronic components onto a standard perfboard. Evaluated on correct component placement, secure electrical connections, and neat board layout.

Focus: Multi-component perfboard routing
Round 3 (Final) 20 Minutes

Final Circuit Challenge

Assemble and solder a complete, functional electronic circuit from given schematics. The circuit is powered and tested for functionality, accuracy, and overall craftsmanship.

Focus: Live circuit functionality & continuity

Expert Guidelines

Official Soldering Tips

  • Appropriate Solder: Use an appropriate amount of solder and make clean, shiny, secure concave joints.
  • Heat Control: Avoid excessive heating of sensitive components (diodes, ICs, transistors).
  • Connection Spacing: Maintain proper spacing between connections to prevent accidental solder bridges.
  • Continuity Check: Check circuit continuity using a multimeter before final submission.

Compliance

Rules & Safety Guidelines

  • Individual participation: Every candidate operates an independent workbench.
  • Time Limits: Participants must complete each round within the allotted time (10m, 15m, 20m).
  • Materials: Components and soldering equipment will be provided at the venue. No external kits allowed.
  • Assistance: No external assistance, cell phone usage, or cross-station communication is allowed.
  • Safety First: Follow all lab safety instructions, use eye protection, and operate equipment only under volunteer supervision.
  • Finality: The judges' decision will be final and binding.
