The Winning Formula

Everyone gets the exact same technical literature passage. You get exactly 2 minutes on the timer.

Highest accurate typing speed wins. In a tie, higher accuracy wins!

Hardware provided at MAT Lab • Zero external keyboards allowed

Format

How It Works

  • Everyone gets the same passage: Standardized benchmark text ensuring a completely fair playing field.
  • Exactly 2 minutes to type: The software timer automatically locks your keystrokes at 120 seconds.
  • Speed is measured in WPM: Words-per-minute calculated in real time using standardized 5-keystroke metrics.
  • Accuracy counts toward the final score: Backspacing is permitted, but uncorrected mistakes penalize net WPM.
  • Highest accurate typing speed wins: Fastest net speed takes the trophy.
Strict Regulations

Rules

  • Individual participation
  • Same text for all participants
  • No internet, external help or copy-paste
  • Use only the computer provided at the venue (MAT Lab)
  • In a tie, higher accuracy wins
  • Coordinator's and timer official's decision is final
