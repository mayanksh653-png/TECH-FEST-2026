Competition 01

Challenge – Coding

A 3-round competition testing programming knowledge, debugging, logical thinking and problem solving. Difficulty rises each round.

Fee: ₹10 (At Desk) Venue: Room 306 Individual 3 rounds C/C++ & Python Only AI tools not allowed Coordinator: Anil Sambyal • 8580699713

The 3 Rounds • Problem Statements (PS) Policy

  • Round 1 • Code Blitz (20–25 min): MCQs & output prediction (PS provided in both C/C++ & Python).
  • Round 2 • Debug the Beast (25–30 min): Find and fix bugs (PS provided in both C/C++ & Python).
  • Round 3 • Final Build (45–60 min): Individual choice • participant picks either C/C++ or Python.

Competition 05 • Practical Electronics

Soldering Competition

Skills • Precision • Practical Electronics. A practical electronics competition to test soldering quality, component identification, connection accuracy, and circuit-building ability.

Fee: ₹20 (At Desk) Venue: Electronics Lab Individual Participation 3 Rounds (45 Min Total) Capacity: 20–30 Coordinator: Ramneek • 8580699713 Prizes for Top 3

The 3 Progressive Rounds (45 Min)

  • Round 1 – Basic Soldering Challenge (10 min): Solder 1–2 components to create a simple circuit. Evaluated on basic technique and neat connections.
  • Round 2 – Perfboard Challenge (15 min): Solder multiple components onto a perfboard. Focus on correct placement and secure connections.
  • Round 3 – Final Circuit Challenge (20 min): Assemble and solder a complete electronic circuit. Evaluated for functionality and finishing.

Evaluation Criteria (100 Marks)

Circuit Functionality: 30 M
Soldering Quality: 25 M
Accuracy & Neatness: 20 M
Component Placement: 15 M
Time Management: 10 M • Total: 100 Marks

Good To Know

Rules you can read before you register.

Clear scoring

Coding scored on test cases and time. Typing on WPM and accuracy. Web development and memes on stated rubric. Soldering on 100-mark hardware rubric.

AI rules stated

AI tools are allowed in Web Development and the Prompt Meme Battle. They are not allowed in the Coding Challenge or Soldering Competition.

Solo or team

Coding, Typing and Soldering are solo. Memes run solo or in pairs. Web Development takes teams up to 4.
